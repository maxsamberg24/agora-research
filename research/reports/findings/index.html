Where these findings come from

  • Primary research — 20 qualitative interviews, ages 23–28, 14 men and 6 women, across NYC, LA, SF, San Diego, Chicago, Syracuse and Atlanta.
  • Instrument — a 7-question survey, every question open-ended.
  • Recruiting — friends and friends-of-friends, which is the sample's main limitation.
  • Standing — directional signal, not statistical proof.
  • Supporting literature — the exposure effect and the roughly 200 hours to a close friendship; Haidt, The Anxious Generation, alongside the critiques of it.

The full report will carry the method, the raw verbatims, and what I would do differently next round.
